Support

Welcome to M3UFlow support. This page provides basic help for the M3UFlow Chrome extension.

Common questions

How do I open a playlist?
Open M3UFlow and select an M3U or M3U8 playlist file from your computer. Pro features may also allow playlists to be opened from a URL.

A playlist opens but a channel does not play.
Check that the playlist address is correct and that the stream is available. Some streams may require authorization or may be restricted by their provider or browser playback compatibility.

Where can I read the privacy policy?
Visit the M3UFlow Privacy Policy.

Contact

If you need further assistance, contact the M3UFlow developer at YOUR_EMAIL.

When asking for help, please describe the issue and include your browser version. Do not send passwords, license keys, or private playlist URLs.
